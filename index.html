About the NGO

Citizens For Animal Protection Foundation

Citizens For Animal Protection Foundation (CAP Foundation) is an animal welfare organization based in Thane, Maharashtra, working towards the care, protection and welfare of animals.

Our visit gave us an opportunity to understand the importance of compassion, responsible animal care and community involvement. We observed how consistent care and small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in the lives of animals.

This website has been created as part of our college NGO project to document our learning and help spread awareness about animal welfare.
